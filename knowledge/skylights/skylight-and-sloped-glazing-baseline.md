# 採光罩與斜面玻璃工程基線

採光罩／sloped glazing 的工程判斷不能直接照搬垂直帷幕牆。重力排水、落物、積水、接縫幾何、坡度與玻璃支承都可能改變風險。

## 主要性能面向

- air leakage
- static / dynamic water penetration
- structural load resistance
- drainage path / end dam / gutter
- glass support and retention
- thermal movement
- condensation and interior drainage
- sealant / gasket durability
- maintenance access and replacement

## 性能試驗 routing

可依系統與專案規範使用 ASTM / AAMA-FGIA 的 air, water, structural test families，例如：

- ASTM E283/E283M — air leakage
- ASTM E331 — static water penetration
- ASTM E1105 — field water penetration
- ASTM E330/E330M — structural performance
- AAMA 501.2-25 — field water check for installed storefronts, curtain walls and sloped glazing
- AAMA 503-24 — field testing of newly installed storefronts, curtain walls and sloped glazing

是否適用到特定 skylight / roof-glazing system，仍須依該標準 scope 與專案規範確認。

## 玻璃設計

玻璃產品規格與結構承載分開處理：

- ASTM C1036 / C1048 / C1172：glass product family
- ASTM E1300：building glass load resistance design practice

斜面玻璃還應特別考慮：
- self-weight
- support orientation
- retained fragments / overhead safety requirement
- ponding / snow / maintenance load（若專案適用）
- glass edge condition and setting

## 水管理原則

採光罩的水密不應只依賴外側一道 sealant。系統設計應先確認：

1. slope 是否足以排水
2. primary drainage path
3. secondary drainage / gutter
4. end dam / splice / corner detail
5. pressure equalization（若系統採用）
6. condensation drainage
7. sealant joint geometry
8. field access for repair

## 實體樣品（Mock-up）／現場試驗注意事項

斜面玻璃的 corner、ridge、valley、gutter、splice 與 wall-to-skylight transition 往往是高風險位置。大型或非典型系統宜將這些 junction 納入 mock-up / field water verification，而不是只測標準直線區段。

## 不可推論事項

- `垂直 curtain wall 水密 PASS = skylight 水密 PASS` 不成立。
- `外側 sealant 完整 = 無需 secondary drainage` 不成立。
- `glass product standard compliant = overhead glazing structural/safety requirement completed` 不成立。
